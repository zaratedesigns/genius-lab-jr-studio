# GENI V2 — Master Character Prompt

**Project:** Genius Lab Jr.  
**Version:** 2.0  
**Status:** Approved

## Character Design

GENI is an adorable, friendly 3D animated inventor robot with a large, separate rounded head and a smaller rounded white torso.

GENI has a glossy white exterior, dark navy face screen, two expressive glowing cyan-blue eyes, blue illuminated ear modules, a small antenna, and a visible illuminated mechanical neck joint connecting the head to the body.

GENI's torso has three vertically aligned glowing blue buttons. His arms are articulated with mechanical joints, and his legs end in oversized futuristic white-and-blue boots.

## Environment

GENI lives in a colorful futuristic science laboratory featuring deep navy backgrounds, electric-blue lighting, magenta and gold accents, holographic planets, floating atoms, scientific instruments, and reflective floors.

## Personality

GENI is curious, intelligent, playful, kind, enthusiastic, and encouraging. He makes science exciting and understandable for children.

## Animation Rules

- Maintain GENI's exact appearance and proportions across scenes.
- Keep the head visibly separate from the torso.
- Preserve the same glowing eyes, three chest buttons, antenna, and mechanical joints.
- Use the approved V2 image as the primary visual reference.
- Maintain a polished cinematic 3D animation style.
- Use smooth, expressive, child-friendly movements.
- Avoid unexpected changes to GENI's colors, anatomy, or accessories.
